I am an MSc candidate at the National University of Singapore with a First-Class BSc (Hons) from the University of Nottingham Ningbo China / University of Nottingham. Having studied in China, the UK, and Singapore, I am interested in how content travels across languages, markets, and communities.

My experience combines three capabilities relevant to global content operations:

1. **Audience and cultural insight** — active interest in K-pop, film, variety content, lifestyle communities, and overseas Gen-Z culture.
2. **Operations and execution** — led 16-20 person teams and delivered community activities from audience insight and promotion through on-site execution and review.
3. **Data and AI** — use R, Python, Excel, SQL, and AI tools to structure information, identify patterns, validate outputs, and communicate findings.
